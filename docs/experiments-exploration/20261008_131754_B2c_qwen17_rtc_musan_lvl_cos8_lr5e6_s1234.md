2. **Epoch 1 did not improve for the first time**: mini WF1 87.69 vs 87.98, noisy EER 12.94 vs 12.52, noisy F1 84.03 vs 84.62, train acc 87.94 vs 88.01. The feature-preservation effect of a lower peak LR is saturated; what 5e-6 buys is a better-behaved late trajectory (dev loss 0.112 vs 0.155, see section 3).

A further halving to 2.5e-6 would make the floor 40 % of the peak and the last three epochs nearly constant-LR, so `--lr 2.5e-6` alone is not the same single factor any more (keeping the ratio needs `--lr_min 5e-7`, a second flag).

Linear extrapolation of the per-ln gains to 2.5e-6 (x0.73 / x0.59 of the last slope, times ln 2) gives **+1.1 to +1.6 WF1**, at the edge of the +-1.5 one-seed band.

### 5.3 Non-speech probes: the default output is "spoof, p ~ 1"

`noise_only` bona-source clips predicted spoof: 54.55 (B0) -> 94.65 (B2a) -> 99.47 (B2b) -> **100.00** (B2c merge and ep8), mean dP(spoof) +0.34 -> +0.69 -> +0.80 -> +0.86 / +0.88; `sil_only` 100 / 100 in every B2 readout (B2a-ep8 was the one checkpoint that said bona). Spoof-source non-speech clips are also 99.9-100 % spoof. What it means: the model treats bona as the *evidence* class (specific speech cues) and spoof as the *default*; a clip without speech evidence gets p_spoof ~ 1 regardless of its source label. It is not a label-correlated shortcut (both sources go the same way) and the probe sets are not part of WF1 (role `probe`), so **it does not matter for the metric directly**. It matters indirectly: it is the limiting case of the SNR curve (heldout R_bona 80.6 at SNR 20 -> 60.5 at SNR 0) and the mechanism of the saturated tail in 5.1 - when noise, g726/gsm or opus:qq mask the bona cues, the output collapses to the default at p ~ 1 rather than to the prior (train prior is 0.81 spoof, i.e. p ~ 0.8). A calibrated default would not change the 0.5 decision for those clips, so the fix is more evidence in degraded bona (data / augmentation / model), not calibration. The iteration-1 secondary rule ("> 50 % -> `--aug_p_apply 0.65` or drop level aug") keeps firing, but the probe tracks the global offset and the LR series monotonically (54 -> 95 -> 99 -> 100 while the augmentation never changed), so it does not implicate the augmentation.

### 5.4 Late-epoch confidence

Train acc 99.50 %, train loss 0.042, thr_eer 1.000 / 0.999 / 0.999 (clean / matched-mini / heldout-mini) at epoch 8; the ep8 tail is slightly larger than the merge's (matched 17.4 vs 16.1 %, heldout 16.9 vs 15.3 % at p >= 0.99) while its F1 is higher - as in B2b.
