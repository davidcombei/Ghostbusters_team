EER is lower in 57 of 63 slice rows (section 2). Clean F1 held (92.37 -> 92.46) and heldout F1@0.5 gained +2.85 even at the biased threshold.
4. **The hardest slices moved most**: gsm +6.26, matched snr 0 +4.96, snr 5 +5.14, wechat +5.05 (matched) / +4.95 (echo), rain +5.10, keyboard +7.43, washing_machine +3.20, clock_alarm +4.66, can_opening +5.07.

7. Wall-clock 4 h 24 min for 8 epochs, inside the <= 10 epoch screen budget.

## 5. What went wrong / open problems (evidence)

### 5.1 A strong, mostly global spoof offset at the 0.5 threshold

- Oracle thresholds (half_B): clean **0.995**, matched **0.942**, heldout **0.879**, echo **0.981**; EER thresholds 0.995-1.000 on every set (B1: 0.68-0.69; B0: 0.31-0.46). R_bona at 0.5: 78.60 / 59.85 / 53.96 / 67.01 vs B0's 87.40 / 79.54 / 71.61 / 85.17 (-8.8 to -19.7); R_spoof 98.25-99.74 (saturated).
- Gap to oracle: WF1 84.95 -> 87.04 (**2.09**). Per set F1@oracle - F1@0.5: clean +1.91, matched +2.43, heldout +1.92, echo +2.88: a similar 2-3 points on every set, which is the signature of a global offset rather than of a set-specific miscalibration.

- Timing: the offset appears at epoch 2 (peak LR) and never reverts (section 3), i.e. it is not produced by the merge or by late epochs; it coincides with train acc 84 -> 93 % in one epoch.

### 5.2 Which slices carry the bona false alarms (B2a @0.5, full sets, R_bona)

- **Language**: en 41.9 (matched) / 36.8 (heldout) / 47.7 (echo) vs zh 72.2 / 63.0 / 77.2. B0 en was 64.3 / 61.2 / 69.0. English F1 is flat vs B0 (+0.86 / +1.03 / +0.69) although English EER improved by 2.7 / 2.5 / 1.4: the threshold eats the whole separability gain on English (train has 14,353 bona vs 61,432 spoof; English bona is the scarce class).
- **Codecs**: g726 40.7 (F1 -3.25 despite EER -4.12), gsm 50.4, g722 70.2; matched qq 47.5, wechat 56.7 (vs voov 74.2, dingtalk/lark 67.2); echo qq 57.5, wechat 62.7.
- **SNR**: matched R_bona 71.0 (none) -> 66.9 (20 dB) -> 63.3 (15) -> 58.1 (10) -> 60.2 (5) -> **47.9 (0)**; heldout 58.3 (20) -> 57.6 -> 54.6 -> 53.2 -> **46.3 (0)**. R_spoof stays 96.5-99.8 across SNR. B0 shows the same monotone SNR trend (87.1 -> 73.5 matched), shifted up by ~16-25 points.
- **Families**: washing_machine 43.6, vacuum_cleaner 45.7 (the only slice whose EER got clearly worse, 26.06 -> 28.64), wind 46.1, door_wood_knock 50.0, door_wood_creaks 51.6 (largest R_bona drop, -26.6); best: glass_breaking 73.4, farend_aec 72.6, echo zoom 79.4.
- **Source**: online bona is worse than offline on every set (57.1 vs 66.2 matched; 51.1 vs 56.8 heldout; 62.2 vs 71.7 echo).

### 5.3 Is the noise_only probe a shortcut?

`noise_only` replaces the whole clip by a held-out noise at the clip's RMS (no speech); `sil_only` zeroes the speech region. B2a predicts spoof for 94.65 % of bona-source and 93.85 % of spoof-source noise_only clips (label-independent, as it must be without speech) and for 100 % of both in sil_only (B0: 100/100 as well; B1 90/95). B0's noise_only was near chance (54.55 / 58.67). So what changed is **the model's default in the absence of speech evidence: it is now "spoof" with p ~ 1** (mean dP +0.69 on bona; B0 +0.34). That is the same global offset as 5.1, not a separate shortcut. Whether "degradation => spoof" is learnt on top of it is answered by 5.1-5.2: the global shift explains two thirds of the gap, and the rest sits in a confidently-wrong tail concentrated in en, g726/gsm, qq/wechat, 0 dB and the stationary-noise families (washing machine, vacuum, wind). Both B0 and B2a lose bona recall monotonically with SNR; B2a's EER on those slices is lower than B0's, so the ranking got better while the operating point got worse.

### 5.4 Other open items

- Only one seed; the +1.33 WF1@0.5 is inside the +-1.5 band (the +2.33 oracle and -2.24 EER are not).
- The merge (epochs 8,6,7,4,5) has never been compared on half_B with the final EMA epoch alone (the protocol's primary readout); `ckpt/epoch_8_dev_loss_0.208444.pth` can be scored with `scripts/local_eval.py --names <17 sets>` at zero training cost.
- Train accuracy 99.24 % against matched-set bona recall 59.85 %: the model fits the training noise pools (coffee 60 files, footsteps 32, keyboard 32, rain 32 files) but transfers only partly to other noise files of the same families.

## 6. Proposed next experiment (iteration 2): `B2b_qwen17_rtc_musan_lvl_cos8_lr1e5_s1234`

**Rules that fire** (plan section 3 rule table, against B2a):
- Rule "train acc > 95 % after warmup" (M3): fires (99.24 %, train loss 0.048, dev loss +85 %, bona recall collapse at peak LR in epoch 2). The EER half of the rule does not fire (EER improves over epochs), but the bias and the confidence tail do appear exactly during the peak-LR epochs.
- Rule "curve still improving at epoch 8, final-epoch EMA is the best epoch": fires (87.09 at epoch 8).
- Rule "thr_oracle > 0.6 on every set, spoof recall > 97 %, bona recall low": fires (0.88-0.995, 98.3-99.7, 54-79), except "EER unchanged" (EER is -2.24).
- Rules on clean-F1 drop, level probes, MUSAN and EMA lag do not fire (clean +0.09; level_flip_bona 3.07; matched/heldout F1 +0.88/+2.85; final EMA epoch is the best).

**Change (one factor): `--lr 3e-5` -> `--lr 1e-5`.** Everything else identical to B2a's command line (head LR becomes 1e-4 via `--head_lr_mult 10`; `--lr_min 1e-6` keeps the same absolute floor, now 10 % of peak instead of 3.3 %). Exact diff versus `config.yaml: command`:

- --lr 3e-5
+ --lr 1e-5
- --track B2a_qwen17_rtc_musan_lvl_cos8_s1234

**Hypothesis.** The spoof offset and the confidently-wrong bona tail are installed by the peak-LR epochs 2-3 (3e-5 -> 2.2e-5), when the backbone fits the training augmentation to 93-95 % in two epochs; the useful learning happened in epochs 4-8 at LR 1.7e-5 -> 1e-6 (WF1 +3.9, EER -2.8). A peak of 1e-5 keeps the whole run in that regime, so the epoch-1 balance (thr_oracle 0.3-0.5, noisy R_bona 72-75) should survive while the annealed tail still delivers the separability.

**Predicted signature** (what must move, and by how much, for the hypothesis to hold):
- Per epoch (`metrics.jsonl`, `train.log`): clean thr_oracle at epoch 2 < 0.9 (B2a 0.95); heldout-mini R_bona never below 60 (B2a fell to 52.6); train acc at epoch 8 in 93-97 % (B2a 99.24), train loss >= 0.10 (B2a 0.048); dev loss not monotonically rising and <= 0.15 at epoch 8 (B2a 0.208); best epoch still among 6-8.
- Merged top-5, half_B: WF1@0.5 >= 85.5 (B2a 84.95); WF1@oracle >= 86.5 (i.e. B2a's 87.04 not lost by more than the band); oracle gap <= 1.5 (B2a 2.09); thr_oracle <= 0.9 on matched and heldout (B2a 0.942 / 0.879); heldout R_bona >= 62 with R_spoof >= 95 **and** heldout EER <= 18 (B2a 54.0 / 98.3 / 17.68: bona recall must come back without the EER rising, otherwise it is a threshold trade and does not count); share of heldout bona at p_spoof >= 0.99 <= 12 % (B2a 23.0 %); noise_only bona-source predicted spoof <= 80 % (B2a 94.65); clean F1 >= 92.0; English heldout R_bona >= 50 (B2a 36.8).
- **Falsified if**: (a) thr_oracle stays >= 0.95 on every set and heldout R_bona < 58 with EER within +-0.5 of B2a -> the offset is not LR-driven; iteration 3 switches to the class prior (`--ce_weights 0.05 0.95`, see below) or augmentation strength. (b) Half_B WF1@0.5 < 83.5 with train acc < 90 % and the WF1 curve still rising at epoch 8 -> under-fit; iteration 3 = `--lr 2e-5` (the rule table's fallback). (c) Noisy EER > 17.0 while thr_oracle normalises -> the peak-LR phase was needed for separability; revert to 3e-5 and use the prior lever instead.

**Ranked alternatives for later iterations (and why they rank below the LR change now):**
1. `--num_epochs 10` (rule "final epoch is the best"): +0.64 WF1 from epoch 7 to 8 on the mini sets (86.45 -> 87.09). Ranked lower because the gains coincide with the LR reaching the floor, not with more steps; re-stretching the cosine adds two epochs at >= 1e-5 while train acc is already 99.2 % and dev loss is rising, and it costs +65 min. Natural follow-up if 1e-5 under-fits.
2. `--ce_weights 0.05 0.95` (more bona weight; direct attack on the 0.5 operating point). Ceiling = the oracle gap (+2.09, of which +1.40 is reachable by a global shift); but with train loss 0.048 the weights only act on the ~0.8 % misfit training clips, so the leverage is uncertain, and by the protocol a pure threshold move (R_bona up, R_spoof down, EER flat) is not an improvement. Note on the plan's rule-table value `0.19 0.81`: train has 14,353 bona / 61,432 spoof (18.9 % bona), so 0.19/0.81 is inverse frequency (bona 4.3x) but it is a **smaller** bona weight than the current 0.1/0.9 (bona 9x) and would push the boundary further toward spoof; if the prior lever is used it has to move toward 0.05/0.95, never toward 0.19/0.81. `0.5 0.5` is in the wrong direction as well.
3. `--aug_p_apply 0.65` or dropping `--use_level_aug`: the clean-F1 rule does not fire (+0.09) and the level probes are clean (3.07); the noise_only result is the same spoof default as the global offset and is not specifically implicated by the probe history (B0 without level aug already had sil_only 100 %). Keep for iteration 4+ if the offset survives the LR and the prior changes.
4. `--ema_decay 0.999`: no EMA-lag signal (the final EMA epoch is the best epoch).
5. Drop `--musan`: matched/heldout F1 improved vs B0 (+0.88 / +2.85); the rule does not fire.
6. Second seed (`--seed 2345`) of the current best: the +1.33 is inside the band, but rules do fire, so the seed re-run is deferred until a screen lands inside the band with no rule firing.

## 7. Decision on the baseline

- **Strict promotion rule** (half_B WF1@0.5 > best + 1.5 with clean F1 drop <= 0.5): **not met**. 84.95 - 83.61 = +1.33 < +1.5; the WF1@0.5 gain is inside the one-seed noise band and on its own is not a result. Clean F1 condition is met (+0.09).
- **Secondary evidence, all outside the band and all in the same direction**: WF1@oracle +2.33, mean noisy EER -2.24, heldout EER -4.26, clean EER -1.85, echo EER -1.52, EER lower in 57/63 slices; per-epoch stability (last-5-epoch range 2.4 vs B0's 7.3 over ten epochs); a usable final-epoch EMA readout. These are separability gains, which is what the protocol counts (a threshold shift alone would not).
- **Decision**: **B2a becomes the reference for iteration 2** - the configuration the next one-factor change is diffed against and the `--baseline` of the next `/post-train-eval` (`SLS_setup/exp/B2a_qwen17_rtc_musan_lvl_cos8_s1234_epoch8_bs16_20261007231952/local_eval/merge_top5`, half_B 84.95). B0 cannot be the parent of a one-factor change (it has none of the recipe), and the plan's loop is defined relative to the current best run. The promotion is **provisional**: its WF1@0.5 lead over B0 needs a second seed to be confirmed, and the strict promotion floor for iteration 2 is 84.95 + 1.5 = **86.45** half_B WF1@0.5 (with clean F1 >= 91.96). `docs/experiment_log.csv` row `B2a-cos8-screen` already records `promote` with this caveat in its notes.
