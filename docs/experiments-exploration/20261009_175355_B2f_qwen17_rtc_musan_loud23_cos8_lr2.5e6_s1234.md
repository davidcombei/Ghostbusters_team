### 2.3 Probes (`probe_report.json`; probe sets of `metrics.json`, 187 bona + 813 spoof; one bona probe clip = 0.53 points)

**What the level probes measure under `--loudness_norm`.** The eight "level" probe sets are built from the same 1,000 `probe_orig` clips (`scripts/build_probe_sets.py`): `gain_down` x 10^-0.5 (-10 dB), `gain_m20` x 0.1, `gain_m6` x 10^-0.3 - pure linear gains; `gain_up` +10 dB **hard-clipped at +-0.99**, `gain_p6` +6 dB clipped at +-0.99, `gain_p10_limit` +10 dB through a soft look-ahead limiter; `agc_dynaudnorm` (ffmpeg `dynaudnorm`) and `loudnorm` (ffmpeg `loudnorm I=-16`) - non-linear dynamics. With `--loudness_norm` every clip is rescaled to -23 LUFS at read time, so a pure gain is undone exactly (up to float32 rounding of the gain and the 400 ms gating): **for B2f, `probe_gain_down` / `gain_m20` / `gain_m6` are the same audio as `probe_orig`**, and the probe report confirms it - bona flip rate **0.00 / 0.00 / 0.00 %** in both readouts, mean dP +-0.001, and in the score files (`scores/probe_*.logits` vs `probe_orig.logits`) r = 1.00000 / 0.99995 / 1.00000 with mean |d logit| 0.012 / 0.054 / 0.013 and 0 flips over all 1,000 clips (B2e-ep8 on the raw audio: r 0.99965 / 0.99801 / 0.99989, mean |d logit| 0.17 / 0.38 / 0.11, 1 / 5 / 1 flips). The clipped and limited probes are **not** undone by the normaliser: clipping at 0.99 and the limiter change the waveform shape, and after re-normalisation to -23 LUFS the difference to `probe_orig` is pure distortion at equal loudness; `finalize` (peak -> 0.99 only if the peak exceeds 1.0) adds nothing at -23 LUFS. These probes still differ for B2f (gain_up: 11 flips of 1,000, mean |d logit| 1.99, r 0.979; gain_p6: 5 flips, 0.72; gain_p10_limit: 6 flips, 0.86; agc 4 flips; loudnorm 2 flips), as they do for B2e (10 / 6 / 3 / 1 / 2). **Consequence: for B2f the summary `level_flip_bona` (mean over the 8 level probes) averages three probes that are 0 by construction with five that measure clipping / limiter / AGC robustness at equal loudness; it no longer measures the level shortcut, and it cannot be compared with B2e's value.** The level shortcut is removed by construction in B2f (the model never sees a level other than -23 LUFS at train or test); what remains testable is the dynamics robustness, below. `probe_orig` itself is loudness-normalised audio for B2f and raw audio for B2e, so the `probe_orig` false-alarm rates are deployment-condition numbers, not same-input numbers.

## 4. What went OK (evidence)

1. **The experiment is clean**: one factor changed (`config.yaml` diff = the level flags, the track name and `--keep_epochs`), same steps, same LR at every step, same seed; `git.diff` contains no code change; the loudness flag is on in training, in both local evals and in the progress-split scoring (flag line in all three logs, `"loudness_norm": -23.0` in both `metrics.json`); the `scores/*.txt` reproduce every `metrics.json` number at threshold 0.5; nothing pruned; 4 h 08 min on one GPU.
2. **The pre-registered rule produced a clean decision** (section 6): final-epoch half_B 92.14 vs 92.53 = -0.39, inside +-0.5 -> null; and the sign is uniform (10 of 10 aggregates, 8 of 8 per-set F1, 8 of 8 per-set R_bona in B2e's favour), so the null is not a "mixed signs" null but a "same model, 0.4 lower" null.
3. **Best half-A numbers of the lineage**: mini WF1 92.80 and mini oracle 93.38 at epoch 8 (B2e 92.64 / 92.97), mini noisy F1 90.90, mini noisy EER 10.38-10.94 at every epoch from 3 on (B2e 10.63-11.17), half-A ahead at 6 of 8 epochs. On the full 4,000-clip sim sets B2f-ep8 is within 0.09 of B2e-ep8 at WF1 (92.53 vs 92.62) and ahead at the oracle (92.98 vs 92.83). The method is not worse than level aug on the available evidence; it is not better either.
4. **Loudness norm removes the level cue by construction, and the probes confirm the mechanism**: pure-gain probes become the same audio as `probe_orig` (0 flips on 3 x 1,000 clips, r(logit) = 1.00000 / 0.99995 / 1.00000; section 2.3). The spoof side tightened (half_B R_spoof matched 99.44 / heldout 99.13 vs 98.81 / 98.63; min slice R_spoof 97.68 vs 96.13; spoof clips with p < 0.5: 9 / 14 vs 19 / 22 on matched / heldout).
5. **Separability is nearly intact where the operating point is not**: oracle -0.14 (ep8) vs WF1 -0.39; echo EER -0.75 and clean EER -0.02 in B2f's favour; a single global threshold at 0.98 recovers 0.40 of the 0.69 oracle gap (section 5.2), i.e. B2f-ep8 at its best global threshold (92.54) equals B2e-ep8 at 0.5 (92.53).
6. **Complementary errors, usable for ensembling**: logit correlation B2f-ep8 vs B2e-ep8 0.990 / 0.977 / 0.971 / 0.983 (clean / matched / heldout / echo) and B2f-ep8 vs B2c-ep8 0.985 / 0.969 / 0.960 / 0.975 - the latter the lowest pair measured in the lineage (B2c-B2e: 0.990 / 0.969 / 0.968 / 0.977). The score average (mean p_spoof) of B2e-ep8 and B2f-ep8 - each scored under its own preprocessing, which is what the score files are - gives half_B WF1@0.5 **92.86** (clean 98.13, matched 91.98, heldout 89.23, echo 95.25; best global threshold 0.925 -> 93.23); B2c-ep8 + B2f-ep8 gives **92.96** (97.86 / 91.85 / 89.88 / 94.36); the three-way average 92.72; the merge pair 92.59. Both pairs beat the better parent by +0.33 / +0.43 with no fitted parameter (the B2c + B2e pair gave 92.90 in the B2e report).
7. **Prediction check of the launcher header**: "removes the level cue by construction" - met (section 2.3); "trains no robustness to AGC / compressor dynamics" - in the right direction (gain_p10_limit 4.81 / 3.21 vs 2.14 / 1.60 %, agc 2.14 vs 0.53 % at ep8), at the 1-5-clip level; "below -> the stochastic dynamics of level aug matter" - the sign is below, but the magnitude is inside the band, so the header's own rule classifies it as null, not as "below".

## 5. What went wrong / open problems (evidence)

### 5.1 Why loudness norm did not beat level aug

- **The level cue was not what limited WF1.** B2e's gain probes already flipped only 0.5-4.8 % of bona (1-9 clips of 187) and its `level_flip_spoof` was 0.02 %: the level shortcut had been removed by level aug to within the probe's resolution. Removing it "by construction" instead cannot buy more than those few clips, and the worst slices are untouched: B2f-ep8's worst list is B2e's (vacuum_cleaner 82.01, heldout snr 0 83.82, heldout en 84.47, washing_machine 84.78, gsm 85.39, qq 86.04, matched snr 0 86.38, matched en 86.71, rain 87.31, g726 88.85 - R_bona 57-70 on all of them). The bona false alarms under unfamiliar degradation, English and SNR 0 do not depend on the level treatment.
- **What loudness norm gives up.** Level aug exposed the model to -38..-12 dBFS, AGC, compression, limiting and clipping on 80 % of the training clips; B2f saw one level and none of the dynamics. The measurable price is small (limiter / AGC probes +3-5 clips; trimmed / padded +5 clips) and the regularisation price shows in the losses (train loss lower at every epoch 2-7, dev loss +14 % at the end and still rising; section 3). The net on half_B is -0.39 / -0.36, on the full sets -0.09 / -0.22, on half A +0.16.
- **B2f sits further on the spoof side at 0.5.** Noisy bona false alarms 26.9 % (ep8) / 27.2 % (merge) vs 24.0 / 25.1 %; matched `thr_oracle` 0.991 vs 0.841 (ep8); calibration gap noisy 0.71 / 0.66 vs 0.53 / 0.54; R_spoof up, R_bona down on every set. The B2e-specific gain - a smaller spoof prior at the same LR - came from the interaction of the low LR with the level-randomised training distribution; with a single input level the prior went back to B2c's level (B2c-ep8 matched thr_oracle 0.987, noisy FA 25.8 %).

### 5.4 Other open items

- **The probe battery needs a loudness-norm-aware variant.** For a `--loudness_norm` model the pure-gain probes are identities and `level_flip_bona` is not comparable with the level-aug runs (section 2.3). A probe set that applies the gain *after* normalisation is impossible by design (the normaliser runs in `_load`), so the right level probe for such a model is the inference-time one: score the report sets with and without the flag and read the drop - which is also the "what if the deployment pipeline forgets the flag" number (not measured here; `scripts/local_eval.py` caches per set name, so it needs a fresh `--out`).
- **Deployment dependency.** B2f requires `--loudness_norm --loudness_norm_lufs -23` in `main_eval.py` for every scoring (the submission zip was produced with it: `model_merging_top5/main_eval.log` line 2) and `pyloudnorm==0.2.0` in the inference environment. Scoring B2f without the flag is a train / test mismatch and is not a valid readout of the model.
- **One seed per configuration, six runs.** Three one-factor changes around this operating point have now given +0.26, -0.42 and -0.39 on the final-epoch readout and up to 4 points per slice (vacuum_cleaner -4.02, wind +4.30, matched snr 0 +4.19); half A and half B of the same 4,000-clip set disagree by ~1 point on the sign. The band for a second seed is still unmeasured and at least this large.
- Registry rows `B2f-loudnorm-screen` (91.98) and `B2f-loudnorm-ep8` (92.14) have an empty `decision` column (section 0 header); the `change_vs_ref` of the screen row lists the merge epochs correctly (8, 5, 7, 3, 4).
- The B2f merge admits epoch 3 (early plateau) because epoch 6 missed the top-5 by 0.03 mini WF1; a last-3 merge (6, 7, 8) was not computed.

## 6. Verdict on the pre-registered falsification rule

`scripts/run_B2f.sh` pre-registered, on the final-epoch half_B WF1@0.5 vs B2e-ep8 (92.53):
- **within +-0.5 -> null (the two level treatments are equivalent)**. Measured: **B2f-ep8 92.14, d = -0.39, inside +-0.5. Null fired.** Secondary readouts agree: merge 91.98 vs 92.34 (-0.36), oracle 92.82 vs 92.97 (-0.14) and 92.59 vs 92.88 (-0.28), full-set WF1 92.53 vs 92.62 (-0.09), half-A mini 92.80 vs 92.64 (+0.16).
- below -0.5 -> "the stochastic dynamics of level aug matter, keep level aug": not fired (-0.39 > -0.5), but the direction is this one on 10 of 10 aggregates and 8 of 8 per-set F1 / R_bona on half B, with the measurable dynamics price at the 3-6-clip level on the limiter / AGC probes and the regularisation price in the dev-loss trajectory.
- above +0.5 -> "promote loudness norm, consider stacking": not fired (no readout is above B2e on half B).
- Formal promotion vs the lineage reference B2c (> +1.5 with clean F1 down by <= 0.5): B2f merge 91.98 - 91.73 = +0.25, final 92.14 - 92.26 = -0.12; not met.

**What it implies.** Deterministic loudness normalisation and stochastic level augmentation are statistically equivalent on one seed for the half_B readout; on the balance of the evidence (half B uniformly 0.1-0.4 lower, spoof prior back to B2c's level, dev loss rising, probe dynamics slightly worse, half A and full-set oracle slightly better) loudness norm is not an improvement over level aug and brings an inference-time dependency with it. The level treatment is not a lever for WF1 at this operating point: both treatments leave the same worst slices (unseen DSP, English, SNR 0, Chinese-vs-English gap) at the same level.

## 7. Decision on the baseline

- **B2f does not replace B2e (nor B2c) as the reference, and `--use_level_aug` stays in the recipe.** Rule: > +1.5 half_B WF1@0.5 with clean F1 down by no more than 0.5; B2f: +0.25 / -0.12 vs B2c, -0.36 / -0.39 vs B2e. Registry decision for both B2f rows: `drop` as a recipe change (null, negative sign), with the note that the run is kept as an ensemble member. B2c's `local_eval/merge_top5` (91.73) and `local_eval/epoch_8` (92.26) remain the formal `--baseline` targets; B2e's `local_eval/epoch_8` (92.53) remains the floor for the full-run seeds. **The full-run configuration stays as recommended in the wave summary (B2e's flags + `--keep_epochs 8`), i.e. with `--use_level_aug` and without `--loudness_norm`**; nothing in B2f changes the seeds-first plan.
- **Why not switch on the tie-break, as was done for B2e over B2c.** B2e-over-B2c was a tie-break in which every aggregate pointed the same way *toward* the candidate; here every aggregate points *away* from it on half B (10 of 10), the only readouts in its favour are half A (+0.16) and the full-set oracle (+0.15), it costs an inference-time dependency (`pyloudnorm`, the flag on every `main_eval.py` call, a train/test mismatch if forgotten), ~0.5 min per epoch, and it makes the level probe battery uninformative. The tie-breakers are one-sided in the other direction.
- **Next step, in order of expected value.**
  1. **Use B2f as an ensemble member, not as a recipe** (zero training, 10 min): the score average of B2e-ep8 and B2f-ep8 gives half_B **92.86** (+0.33 over B2e-ep8), B2c-ep8 + B2f-ep8 **92.96**, the three-way 92.72; the pairs are complementary per set (B2f + B2c keeps B2c's heldout 89.88, B2f + B2e keeps B2e's clean / echo). Each member is scored under its own preprocessing (B2f with the flag), which the existing `scores/*.txt` already are; the progress-split version is the mean of the two `model_merging_top5/scores.txt` files or of two `main_eval.py` runs on the `epoch_8` checkpoints (B2f's with `--loudness_norm --loudness_norm_lufs -23`). This is the same Z-A lever as in the wave summary, with a differently-augmented member instead of a second seed: logit correlation 0.960-0.990 per set, the lowest pairs in the lineage. Weight averaging across the two is not safe (different input preprocessing; the cross-LR weight average already lost 0.4-0.7 in the B2e report) and should not be attempted.
  2. **Stacking `--loudness_norm --use_level_aug` is a legitimate one-factor screen but a low-priority one.** The hypothesis would be "one input level distribution plus the AGC / compressor dynamics": loudness norm fixes the level before the augmenter, level aug then re-randomises it (-38..-12 dBFS) and adds the dynamics, so the stacked model sees the dynamics but gains nothing from the normalisation at train time (the random gain overrides it) and keeps the deployment dependency; what the stack could change is only the eval-side input level, which the level-aug model is already invariant to (gain probes 0.5-4.8 %). Expected effect inside +-0.4; falsification rule if run anyway: final-epoch half_B vs B2e-ep8 92.53, +-0.5 null, with the B2e-ep8 + candidate score average as the secondary readout. Run it only after the full-run seeds, if at all.
  3. **Loudness norm at eval only** is possible for B2e (the flag is a `local_eval.py` / `main_eval.py` option and -23 LUFS is inside the training level range), but it can only re-measure what the gain probes measure (expected |d| < 0.2, 1-9 clips); the reverse - scoring B2f without the flag - is a train/test mismatch and must not be used as a readout. Neither is worth GPU time before the seeds.
  4. What B2f did not move, again, is the next phase's list (wave summary section 5): bona recall under unseen DSP (vacuum_cleaner 57.1, washing_machine 65.4, gsm 65.4, g726 70.3), SNR 0 (61-66), English (58.9-64.3 on half B), and the non-speech probes (100 % spoof). C1 (class / language-balanced sampler), C4 (SNR range to -5 dB) and C2 (paired consistency) stay ahead of any further level-treatment screen.
- **Readout for the full run unchanged: final-epoch EMA checkpoint, half_B WF1@0.5**, protected by `--keep_epochs 8`. Floors: 92.53 (B2e-ep8); band +-0.5; formal 93.76 / 93.23. Expected band for a one-factor change at this operating point, now measured on three near-replicates (B2d, B2e, B2f): +-0.4 on the final readout, 1-2 points per set, 2-4 points per slice.
