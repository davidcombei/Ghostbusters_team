## 4. Lessons (each with the number that proves it)

1. **Paired JS consistency at λ 0.3 is the wave's only positive lever, and it is a ranking gain.**
   - Merge +1.08 [+0.52, +1.67] vs B2f, with clean drop 0.12 (`iter01/verdict.json`).
   - Oracle +0.65 / +0.63; separability gap 7.87 → 6.56 (merge); heldout +2.28 [+1.10, +3.47] (B3a report §2.1).
   - The merge's operating point became optimal at 0.5: best global logit −0.15, and one global shift recovers 0.02 (B2f: +3.10 / 0.41; B3a report §5.2).
   - It moved unseen-DSP heldout bona most: gsm R_bona 65.4 → 73.6 and vacuum_cleaner 57.1 → 71.4 at ep8 (B3a report §2.4).
   - **Confound:** the lever also doubles the optimiser steps (`Warmup-cosine: 3790 warmup / 75792 steps` against B2f's 37,896) and shows two views per clip. The 0.3·JS term is only 2.5-3.9 % of the train loss (B3a report §3). The wave did not separate the three.

2. **More JS is worse. The optimum is at or below 0.3, not above it.**
   - B3c (λ 1.0): merge −0.77 [−1.31, −0.22].
   - The unweighted JS did not fall: 0.0044 vs 0.0038 at ep8 (`metrics.jsonl`).
   - The saturated noisy-bona tail grew: p ≥ 0.99 17.6 → 21.2 %, p ≥ 0.999 9.2 → 14.7 % (B3c report §5).
   - B3c ties B3a on half A (93.01 vs 93.04 merge), so its WF1 loss is half-B-specific. Its heldout EER loss is not: it is worse on both halves.

3. **The English bona gap is not an exposure problem.**
   - The balanced sampler drew each English bona clip ~6.5× per epoch (`iter02/design.result.json`), yet English got worse: half_B English R_bona matched 67.4 → 64.3, heldout 62.0 → 61.2; English FA 91 → 96 (B3b `analyze.result.json`).
   - Spoof under-exposure cost spoof recall: noisy spoof misses 25 → 58.
   - The CE re-derivation did not keep the operating point: best global logit −0.15 → +2.60.
   - Do not repeat the sampler at this recipe.

4. **Every input-side change that made training harder cut the late-training fit and moved heldout bona toward spoof.**
   - B3d (random Opus) cost −2.20, the largest loss of the wave, and it holds on half A (−2.71).
   - B3d's ep1 train acc was 84.75 vs 90.16, and its ep8 CE was 0.0784 vs 0.0283 (B3d report). B3e's ep8 CE was 1.51× B3a's.
   - Heldout carried 61 % of B3d's merge loss, 75 % of B3c's and 55 % of B3e's (reports §2.1).
   - The targeted slices got *worse* in both cases: B3d g726 / gsm R_bona −13.42 / −11.38 (computed from 71.14 → 57.72, 72.76 → 61.38); B3e heldout SNR-0 R_bona −4.09 (computed from 65.31 → 61.22).
   - At 8 epochs and LR 2.5e-6, widening the augmentation distribution (even toward the eval support, as B3e did) is not a free lever.

5. **The saturated core is untouched by anything tried so far.**
   - Noisy bona at p ≥ 0.99 on the merge: B2f 17.4 %, B3a 17.6 %, B3b 15.7 %, B3c 21.2 %, B3d 21.6 %, B3e 18.8 % (§3).
   - `noise_only` and `sil_only` bona-source probes are 99.47-100 % spoof in all 12 readouts (`probe_report.json`).
   - Consistency fixed the movable band (p 0.5-0.99 nearly empty in B3a: matched 21.0 / 21.0 %, heldout 25.1 / 25.1 % at ≥ 0.5 / ≥ 0.9; B3a report §5.3), but not the core.

6. **The remaining error after B3a is ranking, not calibration.**
   - B3a-top5 has 180 of 782 half_B noisy bona as false alarms, 138 of them (77 %) at p ≥ 0.99 (B3e report §5.3).
   - Heldout holds 98 of them: g726 36 / 129, gsm 38 / 127, g722 24 / 135. English heldout bona are flagged at twice the Chinese rate (49 / 129 vs 49 / 262).
   - None of B3b-B3e beats B3a even at its own best global threshold: 92.58 / ~92.39 / ~91.70 / 92.22 against 93.06 (computed from §3).

7. **The selection metric can miss a heldout ranking loss.** B3c's mini WF1 was above B3a's at 5 of 8 epochs (top-5 mean 92.54 vs 92.31), while its mini noisy EER was worse at 6 of 8 (B3c `analyze.result.json`). B3d's mini WF1 tracked half_B (below B3a at all 8 epochs).

8. **The seed band of the consistency recipe is unmeasured.**
   - B3a's 93.06 rests on one seed, and every comparison in B3b-B3e is against it.
   - B3c and B3e lose on half_B but are inside the ±0.5 band on half A (§2.1).
   - The full run's first new seed is therefore also the wave's missing control.

9. **Cost.** Paired consistency doubles the wall-clock: 7 h 43 min to 7 h 59 min per run against B2f's 4.14 GPU h. Epochs take 57.7-59.8 min (per-iteration reports §1). None of the B3b-B3e flags changed the time.

## 5. Recommended full run

"Full run" = more seeds of the best configuration, not more epochs:
- The screens already use the full 75,785-clip train set (`config.yaml` `train_info.n_train`).
- B3a's mini WF1 peaked at epoch 6 and fell by epoch 8 (§2.2), so more epochs are not indicated. Ten epochs were null in the B2 wave (B2d; B2 summary §3).

**Configuration: B3a's flags, unchanged.** It is the only promoted run of the wave (`wave_table.md` final best).
