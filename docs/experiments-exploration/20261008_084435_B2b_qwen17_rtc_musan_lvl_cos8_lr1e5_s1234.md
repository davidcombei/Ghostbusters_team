- **Epoch 1 is bona-leaning, the spoof offset is installed in epoch 2 again, at LR 9e-6**: thr_oracle clean 0.14 -> 0.96, matched-mini 0.22 -> 0.81, heldout-mini 0.40 -> 0.74; R_bona cl/ma/he 93.0/87.2/81.1 -> 87.1/74.9/67.3; R_spoof 98.5/91.4/91.0 -> 99.9/98.8/98.3. ### 5.3 Late overfitting: real in confidence, hidden in the WF1 readout

Train acc 99.51 %, train loss 0.041, dev loss +73 % from epoch 1 to 7 while dev acc is flat (97.3 -> 97.9) and mini WF1/EER are flat from epoch 2. Neither the merge nor the EMA hides it: the per-epoch numbers *are* EMA weights, and the merge is worse than the final EMA epoch. What the WF1 readout hides is that the extra epochs buy only confidence: thr_eer reaches 1.00 on all three mini sets from epoch 5, heldout-mini ECE stays 0.071-0.076, and the p >= 0.999 bona tail is slightly larger for ep8 than for the merge (12.5 vs 9.5 % heldout) although ep8's F1 is higher. The model is not getting worse at ranking within 8 epochs; it is getting more extreme, which is what makes the fixed 0.5 operating point fragile (oracle gap 1.66, best global threshold at logit +3.2).
